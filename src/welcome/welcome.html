Tolerance is on

It leaves your feeds up and blurs the posts built to hook you.

Blockers tend to end up switched off because they're all or nothing. You can turn this one up or down until it helps.

How strict do you want it?

You can change this anytime from the Tolerance icon in your toolbar. Pin it from the puzzle icon so it's easy to find.

What the first week is usually like

Days 1–3: you'll notice the blurred posts constantly, and the urge to peek may get stronger before it fades. That's the hook losing its grip, not a sign it isn't working.

Around day 5: the blurs start to fade into the background.

By day 7: you may find you open your feeds less than before you installed it.

If it's still too much after a few days, turn it down rather than off.

Help show whether it works

Make it yours

Hide or show a topic. Describe something in your own words, like a game you're sick of or vague attention-seeking posts, and choose whether to hide it or always show it on every site. Add a topic

Trust people you want to see. Right-click a post and choose "Trust this author". Their posts stay unblurred until late in a long session.
