Penalize Ads

Gives ads and sponsored posts the top score on every site, so they get blurred. Changing this re-scores posts as they appear after you reload the page.

Blur AI-Labeled Posts

Gives the top score to posts declared AI-generated: labeled by the site ("AI info", "Altered or synthetic content"), tagged by their author (#aiart, "made with Sora"), or carrying an AI generator's watermark. It goes by labels only and does not guess whether unlabeled writing or images were made by AI. Changing this re-scores posts as they appear after you reload the page.

Grounding Breaks

After a stretch of continuous scrolling, offer a short grounding exercise: a few slow breaths, 5-4-3-2-1, or feet on the floor. Always optional. A 5-minute break resets the clock.
